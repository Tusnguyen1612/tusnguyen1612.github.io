    • Analyzed the relationship between urbanization and poverty using a balanced panel of 5,685 households from VHLSS 2012, 2014, and 2016, combined with district-level VIIRS nighttime light data as an objective proxy for urbanization.
    • Constructed Foster-Greer-Thorbecke (FGT) poverty measures (P0, P1, P2) and key household and district controls, then estimated fixed-effects models using Stata’s reghdfe with district, household, and province-year fixed effects.
    • Evaluated linear and non-linear urbanization effects through squared nightlight terms, marginal effects, and predictive margins. Found an inverted-U relationship with poverty depth (P1) at the household level, and no statistically significant effect across poverty measures at the district level.
