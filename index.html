Impact of Urbanization on Poverty in Vietnam

Mar 2026 – May 2026
Stata · VHLSS · VIIRS
  • Built a balanced panel of 1,895 households (5,685 observations) from VHLSS 2012, 2014 and 2016, merged with district-level VIIRS nighttime-light data covering 562 districts and 63 provinces in the regression sample, as an objective proxy for urbanization.
  • Constructed Foster-Greer-Thorbecke (FGT) poverty measures (P0, P1, P2) against GSO-World Bank poverty lines, and estimated fixed-effects models with Stata's reghdfe, using district, household and province-year fixed effects with clustered standard errors.
  • Tested non-linearity through squared nightlight terms and predictive margins: found an inverted-U relationship with poverty depth (P1) at the household level, significant at 5% between districts, with a turning point around 53–59 nW/cm²/sr within the 0–76.8 sample range, but no significant district-level effect across 678 districts and 2,034 observations. Each extra year of schooling was linked to 1.7–2.3 percentage points lower poverty incidence.
github.com/Tusnguyen1612/Urbanization-Poverty-Vietnam
